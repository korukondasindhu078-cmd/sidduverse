2028 — SOME THINGS STAY

Hey, Sidduuuu!

Well, it's 2028 now. Look at us, making it through another year. And look at you, hopefully becoming a little more responsible. I'm still keeping my expectations realistic, though.

I wonder where you are right now, what your days look like and what you've been up to.

College is probably a memory by now. No more rushing to classes, no more familiar corridors, no more seeing the same faces almost every day. No more pretending that assignments will somehow complete themselves the night before submission.

Funny how something that once felt like our entire world eventually becomes just one chapter of life.

I wonder if you ever miss it.

I wonder if you remember the little things as clearly as I do. The random conversations, the unexpected laughs, the silly arguments, the moments when absolutely nothing special was happening, yet somehow they became memories worth keeping.

I think that's what I find most interesting about memories. They don't always stay because something extraordinary happened. Sometimes they stay because of who was there.

And you, Sidduuuu, were there for quite a few moments I'll always remember.

By now, life might have taken us through so many changes. New places, new people, new responsibilities and probably a hundred new things to worry about.

Maybe we're still close. Maybe we're not in each other's everyday lives anymore. Maybe we talk often, or maybe we only occasionally wonder how the other person is doing.

Whatever it is, I hope you're doing well.

And I hope you know that being remembered doesn't always depend on being around all the time.

You'll still be someone I think of with a lot of affection.

Not because I expect anything from you, and not because I want life to remain exactly as it was. Just because some people genuinely matter, and the memories attached to them don't suddenly become meaningless when life changes.

Oh, and of course…

HAPPY BIRTHDAY, SIDDUUUU! ❤️

Another year older. Hopefully wiser too, although we'll need some evidence before confirming that.

I hope this year brings you opportunities you're excited about, people who genuinely value you and moments that make you feel proud of the person you're becoming.

Whatever life looks like right now, I hope you're happy, healthy and enjoying your journey.

Also, I hope you've finally learnt to make decisions without conducting a full investigation first. Especially when it comes to your iPhone. It's been long enough, Sidduuuu. We need progress.

But seriously, I hope you're working towards your dreams, taking care of yourself and finding reasons to enjoy the little things. I hope you have people around you who make you laugh, support you and remind you that life isn't only about getting somewhere.

And if you ever feel like life has become too serious, remember to be a little silly sometimes. You don't have to have everything figured out.

I certainly don't.

There's something I wish I could tell the version of you reading this: don't underestimate the little moments. The people you meet, the conversations you have and the days that seem completely ordinary can become some of the most meaningful parts of your life.

I learnt that in my own way.

And I'm glad I did.

So, here's to wherever life has taken us by now. To the things we've achieved, the things we've outgrown, the memories we've carried and all the new ones we've made.

Some people may no longer be beside us every day, but that doesn't mean they leave our story completely.

Take care, Sidduuuu. Keep growing, keep exploring and please, for once, don't overthink everything.

Some echoes don't ask to be heard. They simply stay.

With warmth, a little mischief, and memories that still make me smile.
