2027 — A LETTER FROM THE PAST

Hey, Sidduuuu!

Well, look at that. It's 2027 already.

I wonder how you are while reading this. What has changed? What's new? Are you still the same old you, or have you finally become a little more sensible? Actually, never mind. Let's not expect miracles.

I don't know where to begin, honestly. A year ago, when I was writing this, we were still in the middle of our college days. The assignments, the exams, the bus rides, the random conversations, the little moments in between everything else.

Back then, they were just ordinary days. We probably didn't even realise how many little memories we were collecting without trying.

And now, I wonder if you ever find yourself remembering them.

Maybe a familiar place reminds you of something. Maybe you come across an old picture and end up smiling at it. Maybe some random little thing brings back a conversation you had completely forgotten.

I hope that happens sometimes.

Because, Sidduuuu, there's something quite beautiful about having people and moments that become a part of your life without you even noticing when it happened.

I wonder where life will have placed us by the time you read this. Maybe we're still together, making new memories and irritating each other as usual. Or maybe life has taken us in different directions, and we're no longer a part of each other's everyday routine.

I genuinely don't know.

But I do know that you'll always be one of the dearest people to me.

And I don't mean that only if things work out between us. I mean it regardless of where we stand by then.

You have been a part of a chapter of my life that I will always hold close. There are little things about you, about us, and about those days that I don't think I'll ever remember as just ordinary.

And if we're still making memories together, I hope we're making plenty of good ones.

If we're not, I hope life has been kind to both of us.

Because somewhere in all those ordinary days, you became someone very special to me. And I'm glad I got to know you, laugh with you, make memories with you and experience those little moments that eventually became such a big part of my story.

And before I forget the most important thing…

HAPPY BIRTHDAY, SIDDUUUU! ❤️

I hope 2027 is kind to you. I hope you get closer to your dreams, discover new things, make wonderful memories and find happiness in places you least expect.

Keep that curiosity alive, take care of yourself and don't forget to enjoy being young.

You have so much ahead of you, and I genuinely hope it's beautiful.

As you move closer to the end of this college chapter, I hope you take a moment to enjoy it. Don't rush through everything. There are some days you'll wish you could revisit, even if you don't realise it yet.

I know I probably will.

So, if there's one thing I want you to do, it's this: don't rush through your life, Sidduuuu. Enjoy it while it's happening.

And if you ever look back at this little chapter of ours, I hope it brings a smile to your face.

Take care of yourself, Sidduuuu. And keep being you — stubborn, occasionally impossible, unnecessarily serious and, unfortunately for me, quite dear.

Some people become memories. Some memories never really become just the past.

With a little affection, a little teasing, and a whole lot of memories.
